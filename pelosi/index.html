          How this was made

          Public records and published reporting only: SEC filings, court records, tax returns, FEC reports, company releases and major news outlets. Where a news site was hard to reach we link a syndicated copy and name the original publisher.

          Net worth figures are estimates by Forbes and Bloomberg as of . They move with stock prices every trading day. “Our math” marks simple arithmetic on the sourced figures.

          Privacy rules

          No street addresses for anyone. Children appear by name only, with no photos. Private relatives are left out. Photos are of adults who are public figures, under the licenses below.
