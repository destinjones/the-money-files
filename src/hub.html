    How it works

    How we follow the money

    Money is hard to hide when it’s on the public record. Here’s the method behind every file.

    1. Start with a name or a brand

      A billionaire, a candy bar, a streaming app. Somebody owns it, and that owner usually has to say so somewhere public.

    2. Pull the public records

      SEC filings show who owns shares. Court records and financial disclosures show settlements, foundations and trades. Property records and reporting show the homes.

    3. Connect the dots and cite them

      Every number gets a link to where it came from, so you can check it yourself. Estimates are labeled as estimates.

    The rules: no street addresses, kids by first name only (and not at all when their names are private), private relatives left out, no gossip or unproven allegations, and nothing here is investment advice.
